## Image and design

Edit, compress and generate images without installing anything.

- [Coolors](https://go.mymarketing.click/ferr-coolors) - Generates color palettes with the spacebar. `free`
- [Dados em gráfico](https://go.mymarketing.click/ferr-dados-em-grafico) - Charts as PNG or SVG from a table, and carousels with PDF and images. `free` `by the author`
- [Foto no tamanho do Instagram](https://go.mymarketing.click/ferr-foto-no-tamanho-do-instagram) - Your photo in the size of the post, the story and the status, without a bad crop. `free` `by the author`
- [Gerador de favicon](https://go.mymarketing.click/ferr-gerador-de-favicon) - Favicon from letters or an image, as SVG with dark mode, ICO, PNG and webmanifest. `free` `by the author`
- [Gerador de fundos](https://go.mymarketing.click/ferr-gerador-de-fundos) - Backgrounds and wallpapers in the exact size of the banner, the stories, the desktop and the mobile. `free` `by the author`
- [Gerador de QR Code](https://go.mymarketing.click/ferr-gerador-de-qr-code) - The QR Code for your link, your Wi-Fi network or your Pix, as SVG and PNG. `free` `by the author`
- [Photopea](https://go.mymarketing.click/ferr-photopea) - Browser image editor that opens PSD, XCF and Sketch. `free`
- [Redimensionar imagem](https://go.mymarketing.click/ferr-redimensionar-imagem) - Any image at the size you need, in pixels or percentage, as JPG, PNG or WebP. `free` `by the author`
- [remove.bg](https://go.mymarketing.click/ferr-remove-bg) - Removes the background from photos; the low-resolution preview is free. `free`
- [Remover fundo de imagem](https://go.mymarketing.click/ferr-remover-fundo-de-imagem) - The AI runs in the browser, the photo is not uploaded and the PNG comes out at the original resolution. `free` `by the author`
- [Squoosh](https://go.mymarketing.click/ferr-squoosh) - Compresses and converts images in the browser, from Google Chrome Labs. `free` `open source`
- [SVGOMG](https://go.mymarketing.click/ferr-svgomg) - Reduces the size of SVG files, with preview. `free`
